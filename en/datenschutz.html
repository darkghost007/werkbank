2. In short

Workbench is an app for tasks, boards and calendars built on Apple Reminders. We do not run servers of our own, and we use no trackers and no analytics tools. Your tasks, projects and comments stay on your device and inside your Apple ecosystem.

The exception is the optional AI assistant: in “API” mode, content is transmitted to the provider you selected. See “AI features” for exactly what is sent. As long as you do not use the assistant, no data is transmitted at all.

3. Data the app processes

The app processes the following data solely to provide its features:

  • Tasks from Apple Reminders (title, notes, due date, priority, recurrence)
  • Events from calendars you select
  • Project structure (project name, board order, visibility) and task comments
  • Task images and person avatars
  • Email addresses you enter to invite people to shared projects

Images

Task images and person avatars are stored locally in the app’s document directory on your device. For shared projects, images sync via CloudKit (encrypted).

Backup

Backup exports (.kanbanbackup) are always password protected. Exporting without a password is not possible; data is encrypted with AES-GCM and the key is derived from your password via PBKDF2 (SHA-256, 100,000 iterations). Without the password a backup cannot be restored – not by us either. Store both the file and the password carefully.

What the app does not collect

  • No analytics or tracking data
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No proprietary accounts or login credentials
  • No data on servers of our own – we operate none

Transmission to a third party happens only if you use the AI assistant in “API” mode or set up API speech input yourself (see “AI features”).

Apple frameworks used

  • EventKit: access to Reminders and Calendars
  • CloudKit: syncing project structure, comments and images
  • StoreKit: managing in-app purchases (premium features)
  • Speech: speech recognition for quick capture
  • FoundationModels: AI assistant (Apple Intelligence, on device)

Children

The app is intended for users aged 4 and up, contains no advertising and records no user behavior. It does contain in-app purchases and links to external websites (terms of use and this privacy policy). The AI assistant in API mode requires deliberate setup with your own API key.

4. iCloud and sync

Project structure and comments (CloudKit)

Project metadata (project name, board order, visibility) and task comments sync via Apple’s CloudKit framework. This data resides in your iCloud account and is encrypted.

  • Private database: personal projects and comments
  • Shared database: shared projects, comments and permissions

Calendar display (iCloud key-value storage)

Which projects and calendars the calendar shows is synced between your devices via Apple’s iCloud key-value storage. Only internal identifiers (IDs) of projects and calendars and display switches are stored, no content. The data resides in your iCloud account; without iCloud it stays on the device only.

API key

A stored AI API key is synchronized end-to-end encrypted with your other devices via iCloud Keychain (see “AI features”).

5. Calendar and Reminders

Apple Reminders (EventKit)

The app accesses your Apple Reminders to display them as boards, lists and calendar views. Task data (title, notes, due date, priority, recurrence) syncs through your existing Apple accounts (e.g. iCloud, Exchange). Workbench does not store this data on its own servers. Task data in Apple Reminders persists when you delete the app – remove it through the Apple Reminders app.

Calendars (EventKit)

If you grant calendar access, the app reads events from the calendars you select in order to show them in the calendar and weekly views. Access is optional and can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings.

6. AI features

The AI assistant is an optional feature. As long as you do not use it, no data is transmitted at all.

Two modes

  • Apple (Apple Intelligence): The assistant uses Apple’s Foundation Models and, in the current version, runs directly on your device. Requires a device with Apple Intelligence enabled. No project data is transmitted to us. Only if Apple Private Cloud Compute is active in the app (not the case in this version): Apple then preferably processes your request (message, history and the project context needed for it) on Private Cloud Compute on Apple servers. Apple does not store these requests or make them accessible to anyone, including us. When offline or after reaching the daily limit, the assistant then runs directly on your device.
  • API (provider of your choice): You select an OpenAI-compatible provider yourself and store your own API key. Without a stored key this mode cannot be used – nothing is transmitted.

Explicit consent before the first transmission

Before the app sends data to a third-party AI provider for the first time, it shows a consent dialog: to which address the data goes, which data it is (see below), for what purpose, and that the provider’s privacy policy applies. Nothing is sent until you agree. If you decline, the assistant cancels the request and transmits nothing.

  • Consent applies per provider address (scheme, host, port). If you switch provider, the app asks again.
  • If the consent text changes materially, the app asks again.
  • Consent is stored on the respective device only and is not synced via iCloud. On another device you are asked again – even if the AI settings were synced there.
  • You can revoke consent at any time in the AI settings. After that, the app sends nothing to that provider until you consent again.
  • Local servers on your own network (e.g. LM Studio, Ollama) do not count as third-party providers; no consent is requested for them.
  • The connection test and loading the model list in the AI settings send no content from the app (only a fixed test text or a bundled test recording, plus your key for that provider); no consent is requested for them.

Provider choice and responsibility

The presets offered are: OpenAI, OpenRouter, Mistral, Google Gemini, Groq, Fireworks, Together, xAI (Grok), Cerebras, DeepSeek, Mammouth, Ollama Cloud, and a freely configurable endpoint (“Custom”). You can also enter local endpoints on your own network (LM Studio, Ollama); in that case the data does not leave your network.

You choose the provider yourself and enter into the usage relationship directly with them. The privacy policy and terms of that provider govern the processing of the transmitted data. Please read them before using the assistant in API mode.

What is transmitted in API mode

With every request, a system prompt, the conversation history of that chat, and a context excerpt of your data are sent to the provider. The context excerpt may contain:

  • Project names with internal ID plus counts of open tasks and boards (up to 50 projects)
  • Board names with internal ID, frequent tags and sample task titles (up to 20 boards)
  • Titles of open tasks (up to 20 by default)
  • Up to 30 tasks including title, internal ID, board and project name, an excerpt of the notes (up to 140 characters), tags and the names of assigned people
  • Names and internal IDs of people from your projects and shared projects (up to 30)
  • Frequently used tags
  • In the weekly view additionally: titles and identifiers of events from the selected calendars (up to 5 per day) and the titles of planned tasks
  • Your own chat input
  • Results of the tools the assistant calls during a request – for example tasks found or created, with due dates; if you have granted the assistant calendar access, also calendar names and titles and times of events

Only text is transmitted. Images, avatars and file attachments are never sent to the provider.

Speech input

  • Apple dictation (default): Speech recognition runs on your device through Apple’s Speech framework (SpeechTranscriber; SFSpeechRecognizer as a fallback, also restricted to on-device recognition). iOS downloads the required language model from Apple when needed; the recording itself does not leave your device. No audio is sent to us or to your AI provider.
  • Speech input via API (optional): If you switch speech input to your own transcription endpoint, the raw audio recording is uploaded to that endpoint as an audio file and therefore leaves your device – even when the AI assistant runs in “Apple” mode; the app points this out in Settings. If the endpoint is not on your own network, the app asks for your explicit consent before the first upload – separate from the consent for the AI assistant, per server address and per device, revocable at any time. Without consent the recording is recognized on the device. You can store a separate key for this; if none is set, the chat provider’s key is used only when both services run at the same address. If a required key is missing or the upload fails, the recording stays on the device and can be recognized there.

API key

Your API key is stored in your device’s keychain and synchronized end-to-end encrypted with your other devices via iCloud Keychain. It is used solely to authenticate against the provider you selected and is never transmitted to us. The key is bound to the provider address you stored it for: if you switch provider, it is not sent to the new provider – Settings then show that a key is missing for that provider.

A key is never sent over unencrypted HTTP to servers on the internet. To your own server on the local network or VPN that only speaks HTTP, the app sends it only if you explicitly turn this on for exactly that address – key and content are then transmitted unencrypted and can be read by other devices on the same network.

Turning it off

You can switch the assistant back to “Apple” at any time in Settings, revoke consent for the provider, or delete the stored key. After that, no data is transmitted to any provider. Data already transmitted to a provider cannot be retrieved by us – please contact that provider directly.

7. Sharing with others

Email addresses are used solely to send CloudKit sharing invitations for shared projects. They are not stored on our own servers. Invitations are processed through Apple’s CloudKit infrastructure.

Shared projects, comments, permissions and – for shared projects – images reside in the shared CloudKit database and are visible to the people you invite.

8. Diagnostic data

The app keeps a local diagnostic log on your device. Crash and hang reports provided by iOS (MetricKit) are added to it. The log is not transmitted to us automatically; it only leaves your device if you pass it on yourself via “Send diagnostics” in the app. The standard export contains no task content.

9. Purchases

The app contains in-app purchases: Premium as a one-time purchase and as a yearly and a monthly subscription, plus a one-time free 14-day trial. Everything is handled through Apple’s StoreKit. We receive no payment data; purchase status is verified through your Apple account.

10. This website

This website sets no cookies, uses no tracking or analytics tools and loads no external fonts or scripts. It is served via GitHub Pages; the hosting provider (GitHub, Inc., USA) may process technically necessary connection data such as your IP address in server logs. We have no influence on this and do not receive that data. GitHub’s privacy policy applies.

If you email us, we process your message and sender address to reply to you.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Because we store no user data on servers of our own, most of your data lives on your device and in your Apple account, where you can manage it yourself. For requests, write to werkbank-app@pm.me.

Data deletion

  • Local data: Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data (images, avatars, settings).
  • CloudKit data: Shared project data remains in your iCloud account until you delete the project in the app or manage your iCloud account.
  • Reminders: Task data in Apple Reminders persists – remove it through the Apple Reminders app.
  • API key: Delete the key in the AI settings; it is then also removed from iCloud Keychain.

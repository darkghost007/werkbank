2. In short

Workbench is an app for tasks, boards and calendars. We run no servers of our own and use no trackers and no analytics tools. Your tasks, projects and comments are stored on your device and in your iCloud account with Apple. We have no access to them.

Data is only sent to services other than Apple if you set this up and agree: to an AI provider of your choice ("Own API" mode) or to a transcription server for speech input. Until you do, no data goes to such services.

4. Data the app processes

The app processes the following data solely to provide its features:

  • Tasks and subtasks (title, notes, due date, duration, priority, recurrence, tags, status)
  • Projects, boards, templates and your settings
  • Task comments, including @mentions
  • Task images, file attachments and person avatars
  • People in your projects: name, avatar if any, and for invited members the iCloud participant details provided by Apple; for external contributors without iCloud that you add yourself, name and note
  • Email addresses you enter to invite people to shared projects (invitation handled by Apple)
  • Events from the calendars you select; reminders from Apple Reminders if you import or link them

If you enter data about other people, such as contributors' names, you usually do so for private purposes. Please only add what collaboration requires.

Images and file attachments

Images, file attachments and avatars are stored on your device and synced via CloudKit in your iCloud account. In shared projects, members can see them.

Backup

Backup exports (.kanbanbackup) are always password protected. Exporting without a password is not possible. Data is encrypted with AES-GCM, and the key is derived from your password via PBKDF2 (SHA-256, 100,000 iterations). Without the password a backup cannot be restored, not even by us. Keep both the file and the password safe. You decide where the file is stored.

Spotlight, Siri, widgets and notifications

  • Spotlight: Task and project titles are added to your device's local search index so you can find them in iOS search.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: If you use Workbench actions with Siri or Shortcuts, Apple processes your voice request according to your Siri settings. Workbench only receives the result, for example the title of a new task.
  • Widgets: For widgets, the app stores a selection of your tasks and events in a shared container on your device.
  • Notifications: Reminders and briefings are scheduled as local notifications on your device. Apple's push service only delivers the signal that something changed in iCloud.

What the app does not collect

  • No analytics or tracking data
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No proprietary accounts or login credentials
  • No data on servers of our own, as we run none

Apple frameworks used

  • CloudKit: storing and syncing your data in your iCloud account, sharing
  • EventKit: access to calendars and reminders
  • StoreKit: in-app purchases
  • Speech: on-device speech recognition
  • FoundationModels: AI assistant (Apple Intelligence on device)
  • MetricKit: crash and hang reports from iOS, evaluated locally
  • App Intents, Core Spotlight, WidgetKit, UserNotifications

Children

The App Store age rating describes the app's content. It does not define a target audience. Workbench contains no advertising and does not record usage behaviour. Children under 16 can consent to a transfer to third-party AI or transcription services only with parental consent (Art. 8 GDPR). These services often have their own age limits.

5. iCloud and sync

Your data in CloudKit

Tasks, projects, boards, comments, people, images, attachments, templates, tags and settings are stored on your device and synced with your iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit.

  • Private database: your own projects and data. Only you have access.
  • Shared database: projects you share or were invited to, including comments, images, attachments and permissions. The project's members have access.

Apple encrypts this data in transit and at rest. Apple manages the keys unless you have turned on Advanced Data Protection for iCloud. We as the provider cannot see this data.

Without an iCloud sign-in, projects cannot be created or synced.

Calendar display (iCloud key-value store)

Which projects and calendars the calendar shows is synced between your devices via Apple's iCloud key-value store. Only internal identifiers (IDs) of projects and calendars plus display toggles are stored, no content. The data resides in your iCloud account. Without iCloud it stays on the device only.

API key

A stored AI API key is synced end-to-end encrypted to your other devices via iCloud Keychain (see "AI features").

Apple as recipient

Apple provides iCloud services under your agreement with Apple and is responsible for them itself (in the EU: Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland). Apple may also process data in the USA. Apple Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (European Commission adequacy decision, Art. 45 GDPR). More: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

6. Calendar and Reminders

Calendars (EventKit)

If you allow calendar access, the app reads events from the calendars you select and shows them in Today, Week and Calendar. At your request it also creates events. Access is optional and can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings.

Apple Reminders (EventKit)

If you allow access, the app can import reminders from Apple Reminders and link them to tasks. Imported tasks are then stored in Workbench (see section 5). The reminders themselves stay in Apple Reminders, even if you delete the app.

7. AI features

The AI features are optional. They include the AI assistant, suggestions and briefings, and "Write with AI" for notes. The AI makes suggestions but changes nothing without your confirmation. AI results can be wrong. Check them before you apply them. In the app, AI results are labelled as such ("AI-generated – may contain errors"), for example for note drafts, the short summary in the briefing and the AI recommendation.

Two modes

  • Apple (Apple Intelligence): The AI uses Apple's Foundation Models and, in the current version, runs directly on your device. It requires a device with Apple Intelligence turned on. No project data leaves your device. Apple Private Cloud Compute is not active in this version. Should that change, we will update this policy beforehand.
  • Own API (provider of your choice): You select an OpenAI-compatible provider and store your own API key. Without a key and without your consent nothing is transmitted.

Explicit consent before the first transfer

Before the app sends data to a third-party AI provider for the first time, it shows a consent dialog. It names the address the data goes to, the data itself (see below) and the purpose. It also states that the provider's privacy terms apply and that the provider may process data outside the EU, and it links to this privacy policy. Only if you agree is anything sent. If you decline, the app cancels the request and transmits nothing.

  • Consent applies per provider address (scheme, host, port). If you switch providers, the app asks again.
  • If the consent text changes materially, the app asks again.
  • Consent is stored only on the respective device and is not synced via iCloud. On another device you will be asked again, even if your AI settings were synced there.
  • You can revoke consent at any time in the AI settings (Art. 7(3) GDPR). After that the app sends nothing more to that provider until you consent again. Transfers made before revocation remain lawful.
  • Local servers on your own network (e.g. LM Studio, Ollama) do not count as third-party providers. The app does not ask for consent for them.
  • The connection test and loading the model list in the AI settings send no content from the app, only a fixed test text or a bundled test recording and your key for that provider. The app does not ask for consent for these.

Provider choice, responsibility and third countries

The presets offered are: OpenAI, OpenRouter, Mistral, Google Gemini, Groq, Fireworks, Together, xAI (Grok), Cerebras, Mammouth, Ollama Cloud, and a freely configurable endpoint ("Custom"), which you can also use for other OpenAI-compatible services. Local endpoints on your own network are possible too (LM Studio, Ollama). In that case the data does not leave your network.

You choose the provider yourself and enter into the usage relationship directly with them. The provider processes the data under its own responsibility, privacy policy and terms. Please read them before using "Own API" mode. We do not receive the transmitted data.

Many providers are based or process data outside the EU, especially in the USA. Depending on the provider:

  • Providers with an EU contracting entity or EU standard contractual clauses: OpenAI contracts with EEA customers through OpenAI Ireland Ltd. and protects transfers outside the EEA with standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).
  • Providers without an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards: This may include providers in countries for which the EU has not found an equivalent level of data protection. There, authorities may be able to access data without you having effective legal remedies. A transfer then relies solely on your explicit consent (Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR).

You can check whether your provider is certified under the Data Privacy Framework at https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov/list.

What is transmitted in "Own API" mode

With every request to the assistant, a system prompt, the conversation history of that chat and a context excerpt of your data are sent to the provider. The excerpt may contain:

  • Project names with internal ID plus counts of open tasks and boards (up to 50 projects)
  • Board names with internal ID, frequent tags and sample task titles (up to 20 boards)
  • Titles of open tasks (up to 20 by default)
  • Up to 30 tasks with title, internal ID, board and project name, a notes excerpt (up to 140 characters), tags and the names of assigned people
  • Names and internal IDs of people from your projects and shared projects (up to 30)
  • Frequently used tags
  • In the weekly view additionally: titles and identifiers of events from the selected calendars (up to 5 per day) and titles of planned tasks
  • Your own chat input
  • Results of the tools the assistant calls during a request, such as tasks found or created with their due dates. If you have allowed the assistant to use your calendar, also calendar names and event titles and times.

For suggestions and briefings, excerpts of your tasks and events are sent to the provider so it can phrase suggestions. These are counts (events, tasks, overdue items), shortened titles of individual tasks and events with time, due date and flags such as "overdue" or "high priority", and, for filing, project and board names. Tasks from shared projects, notes, comments and attachments are not included. For "Write with AI", the task title, its subtasks, project and board name, the date and the existing note text are sent.

Only text is transmitted. Images, avatars and file attachments are never sent to the provider.

Speech input

  • Apple dictation (default): Speech recognition runs on your device through Apple's Speech framework (SpeechTranscriber, with SFSpeechRecognizer as a fallback, also restricted to on-device recognition). iOS downloads the required language model from Apple when needed. The recording itself does not leave your device. Neither we nor your AI provider receive any audio.
  • Speech input via server (optional): If you switch speech input to your own transcription server, the raw audio recording is uploaded to that server as an audio file and leaves your device. This also applies when the AI assistant runs in "Apple" mode. The app points this out in Settings. If the server is not on your own network, the app asks for your explicit consent before the first upload. This consent is separate from the one for the AI assistant, applies per server address and per device, and can be revoked at any time. Without consent the device recognizes the recording itself. You can store a separate key for the server. If none is set, the chat provider's key is used only when both services run at the same address. If a required key is missing or the upload fails, the recording stays on the device and is recognized there. For third countries, the information under "Provider choice" applies.

API key

Your API key is stored in your device's keychain and synced end-to-end encrypted with your other devices via iCloud Keychain. It is used solely to authenticate with the selected provider and is never transmitted to us. The key is bound to the provider address you saved it for: if you switch providers, it is not sent to the new provider. Settings then show that a key is missing for that provider.

A key is never sent over unencrypted HTTP to servers on the internet. To your own server on a local network or VPN that only speaks HTTP, the app sends it only if you explicitly turn this on for exactly that address. Key and content are then transmitted unencrypted and can be read by other devices on the same network.

Turning it off

You can switch the AI back to "Apple" in Settings at any time, revoke consent for the provider or delete the stored key. After that, no data is transmitted to any provider. We cannot retrieve data already transmitted. Please contact the provider directly.

8. Sharing with others

When you share a project, you invite people through Apple's iCloud sharing, for example by email address or link. Apple handles the invitation. We do not receive or store these details.

Shared projects, including tasks, comments, images, attachments, people and permissions, are stored in the shared CloudKit database. Invited people can see them, as well as your name as Apple displays it for participants. If someone leaves a project, their comments remain visible to the others.

9. Diagnostic data

The app keeps a size-limited diagnostic log on your device. Older entries are overwritten. Recording can be turned off in Settings. Crash and hang reports provided by iOS (MetricKit) are added to it. The log is never transmitted automatically. It only leaves your device if you share it yourself via "Send diagnostics" in the app. The default export contains no task content unless you explicitly turn that on for this export. If you send us the log, we use it only to find the reported bug and delete it afterwards (see section 13).

10. Purchases

The app contains in-app purchases: Premium as a one-time purchase and as a yearly and a monthly subscription, plus, for the yearly subscription, an introductory offer with a 14-day free trial (once per Apple account, where offered in the App Store). Sale and payment are handled by Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland) under Apple's terms. We receive no payment data and no personal details about you. The app checks on your device via StoreKit whether Premium is unlocked.

13. Retention and deletion

  • Data in the app and in iCloud: kept until you delete it in the app. To remove local data, delete the app. To delete Workbench's CloudKit data, go to iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › Workbench (the wording may differ by iOS version).
  • Shared projects: if you delete a project you own, it is removed for all members. If you leave someone else's project, it disappears from your devices.
  • Reminders and events: remain in Apple Reminders and Calendar. Remove them there.
  • API key: delete it in the AI settings. It is then also removed from iCloud Keychain.
  • Consents for third-party AI/transcription: stored locally on the device until you revoke them or delete the app.
  • Diagnostic log: local, overwritten on a rolling basis. Logs you send us are deleted once the bug is fixed, at the latest after 12 months.
  • Emails: deleted once your request is settled, at the latest after 12 months, unless statutory retention obligations apply (e.g. 6 years for business letters under § 257 German Commercial Code / § 147 German Fiscal Code).
  • At AI or transcription providers: according to their rules.

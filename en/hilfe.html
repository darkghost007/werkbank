Sync

How does Workbench sync between my devices?

Through your private iCloud database. Sign in with the same Apple ID on all devices and enable iCloud for Workbench. Changes usually appear within a few seconds.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Everything is saved on the device first. “Offline · sync later” appears at the top; as soon as you are back online, Workbench syncs automatically.

The sync indicator shows an error. What now?

Tap the red indicator. It shows the reason and the last successful sync. The most common cause is full iCloud storage. “Try again” restarts the sync; your changes stay safe on the device.

Sharing

How do I share a project?

Open the project and tap the people icon at the top, or “…” › Members. From there you invite people or manage sharing. Sharing your own projects is part of Premium; invitees don’t need Premium.

How do I change roles or remove someone?

In the sharing sheet, tap the person: choose “Read only” or “Read and write”, or “Remove from project”. Only the project owner can do this.

How do I leave a shared project?

People icon in the project header › Sharing › “Leave project”. The project disappears from your devices; your comments stay visible to the others.

AI setup

Which AI does Workbench use?

By default, Apple Intelligence right on your device. Alternatively you can use your own API key or a local server on your home network. See the step-by-step guide under AI setup.

Does the AI change my tasks on its own?

No. Every suggestion appears as a list for you to confirm. Nothing changes until you tap “Apply” – and even that can be undone.

Premium

How do I restore my purchase?

Settings › Premium › “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. With Family Sharing, the organiser must have shared the purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Manage subscriptions in Workbench under Settings › Manage subscription (shown only with an active subscription) or in iOS Settings under your name › Subscriptions. After cancelling, Premium stays active until the end of the paid period; all your data is kept.

Export

How do I export my data?

Settings › Data › Backups › “Export backup”. The file is encrypted with a password of your choice and can be saved via the share sheet, e.g. to Files.

Can I share a single project as text?

Yes. In the project, “…” › Share as text or Markdown, to paste into Notes, Mail or other apps.
